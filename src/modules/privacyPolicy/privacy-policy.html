1. About this policy

MuscleDesk is a gym-management service used to maintain gym, member, trainer, membership and fee records. This policy describes information handled by the current mobile app, its backend and its account-deletion page. “We”, “us” and “our” refer to the operator of MuscleDesk.

The initial release is planned for India only, based on the operator's instructions for this draft. Other launch countries are not assumed. This market choice does not establish where the service providers store or process data.

MuscleDesk's publisher/operator is Raj. For privacy and support inquiries, contact pcterajbca7772@gmail.com. These details were confirmed by the operator for this draft. The Android package candidate for the first Play release is com.semgroup.gymmanagement; its Play Console availability/history still needs confirmation.

A gym can enter information about people who do not themselves have a MuscleDesk login. The gym determines the records it enters and the staff it authorizes. Contact your gym about its use of your member or trainer record, or use the privacy contact in this policy for service-level questions and requests.

2. Information handled by MuscleDesk

  • Account and gym information: Account email address, name, account status, password verification information, gym name, gym contact email/phone where supplied, optional owner phone/photo, gym timezone, and identifiers linking accounts to gyms and roles. Owner signup requires account and gym details; profile editing supports additional contact information.
  • Member and trainer records: Member/trainer codes, first and last names, phone numbers, status and gym association. Optional supported fields include date of birth, gender, postal address, emergency-contact name/phone/relationship for members, and trainer specializations. Linked login-account identifiers and trainer/member assignments are stored where those features are used. An emergency contact's details may be entered by someone else.
  • Membership and financial records: Plan names/descriptions, price and duration, membership start/end dates, renewal relationships, membership status, cancellation time/reason, notes, payment amounts and dates, payment method, transaction reference, outstanding balances and the account that recorded a payment. Currency amounts and payment history are used for fee administration, dues and revenue summaries.
  • Optional photographs: Photos deliberately selected through the camera or system photo picker for owner/member identification, local selection/recovery information, and server-side image references/revisions. Photographs can identify the person shown. The photo feature does not implement facial recognition or biometric matching.
  • Authentication and recovery records: Passwords provided for signup/sign-in or supported verification, backend password hashes, sessions/tokens, and registration/payment/renewal/photo operation snapshots and receipts. Some recovery records contain personal or financial fields entered for the original operation. New locally implemented password recovery stores an account-linked code digest, registered-email digest, credential revision, expiry and use time; raw codes are sent through the selected mail delivery mechanism rather than stored in the recovery database.
  • Deletion requests: The requesting account reference, request identifier, status and timestamps. For in-app and password-verified web intake, the password is verified and is not stored in the request. If you choose the email-request option, your sender address and request message are handled through email; sending an email does not itself create a backend request reference or verify ownership. New internal manual-fulfillment code records the assigned reviewer, protected verification/ownership/retention/backup evidence references, category retention decisions, scoped cleanup identifiers and outcome evidence. This code has been tested with synthetic accounts only and is not a deployed or approved production fulfillment procedure.
  • Technical and operational information: Server-generated record/request identifiers, creation/update times, request method/route template, response status, processing time, safe error categories and security events. Some backend security events include internal account/session or gym/resource identifiers. The service's network infrastructure necessarily processes connection information such as IP addresses; hosting, proxy, database and provider logs may handle additional technical information.

Free-text notes, descriptions and cancellation reasons contain what the person entering them supplies. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information, payment credentials or passwords into these fields. The app does not implement card-number, CVV, bank-login or payment-gateway collection: a recorded payment method/reference is a gym bookkeeping record, not an in-app transfer of money.

Attendance and branch management are retired features. If a gym used earlier versions, historical attendance or branch-related records may remain; the scope cleanup did not automatically erase historical database records. Their presence and retention must be included in any applicable data review.

3. Where the information comes from and why it is used

Information comes from account owners, authorized gym staff, people using supported account/profile features, deliberately selected photos, and the service's operation. We use it to provide the implemented functions:

  • Create and authenticate accounts, restore sessions, enforce gym/role permissions and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Maintain member/trainer profiles and assignments; identify records and show the profile images a user selected.
  • Administer plans, memberships, expiry, renewal and cancellation, and present membership eligibility and status.
  • Record fees and payment history; calculate dues and revenue; prevent duplicate financial operations and recover uncertain or interrupted requests.
  • Maintain operational/security records, diagnose failures and receive account-deletion requests for manual review.

The current app is not a medical-record, workout-sensor or exercise-tracking product. No Health Connect/HealthKit, precise-location, phone-contact import, SMS, microphone recording, advertising-ID or behavioral-analytics integration was found in the inspected implementation. Postal addresses entered in profiles are separate from device GPS location.

The operator's stated basis for this draft is use for gym-management operations, with disclosure to hosting and photo-storage providers where needed to provide those functions. The inspected app/backend use the services described below; this statement does not establish that no other business sharing occurs.

Confirmation required — additional business uses and sharing: The operator has directed necessary gym operations and essential service-provider sharing only, without advertising or unrelated sharing. No ad/analytics integration was found in the inspected dependencies. Actual provider terms, off-app tools, exports and business practices still need verification; the intended restriction is not proof that every external practice already conforms.

4. Device storage, photos and permissions

The app stores the rotating sign-in refresh credential through platform secure storage. Access tokens are held in memory. Registration and financial recovery records use the existing scoped secure-storage mechanism. These recovery records may remain until their operation has been confirmed and explicitly acknowledged or resolved, so that restarting or signing out does not cause duplicate registration or payment collection.

Photo selections and recovery metadata are kept separately in the app's private application-support files. These files are not the same as the credential secure store, and this policy does not claim additional app-level encryption for every local photo/file. Original selected copies or picker/OS caches may contain metadata. Normal photo completion/discard paths attempt to remove the app's staged copy; interrupted operations or cleanup failures can retain it for recovery.

Photos are optional. Camera or photo-library access is initiated when a user chooses that feature, subject to the device/system picker's access controls. The app does not implement background scanning of the photo library. Denying photo access does not require a profile photo; the existing initials fallback remains available. Device permissions can be managed in system settings.

The backend receives the selected image, validates and re-encodes it to a resized JPEG, and uploads the processed copy to Cloudinary as an authenticated asset. That processed copy does not retain the original EXIF location metadata or embedded profiles. This does not mean metadata was absent from an original temporarily held on the device or received for processing. Image delivery uses the protected API, with revision/cache behavior. Replacement/removal uses the existing cleanup process; cleanup failures can require retry rather than immediate erasure of every copy.

Signing out clears the sign-in credential through the supported logout flow; it does not erase the gym's server records or necessarily remove all unresolved local recovery data. Device/OS backups, caches and provider copies require separate retention and deletion consideration. Uninstalling the app is not a server-account deletion request.

5. Access to data and service providers

Gym records can be accessed through the service by authenticated people whose gym scope, role or assignment permits that access. The operator and authorized people administering the service may need access to support and operate it. A gym's own disclosure or export of records outside MuscleDesk is a separate practice that must be explained by that gym and, where relevant, by the operator.

The current implementation uses or relies on:

  • API hosting: The owner has identified Hostinger VPS as the API host. The backend processes account, gym and operation data there. The actual hosting account, services, processing locations and operational logs must be confirmed before publication.
  • Database storage: MongoDB stores the application's account, gym, member/trainer, membership, financial, operation and request records. MongoDB is the database technology; the actual database hosting provider and location have not been verified. This draft does not assume MongoDB Atlas is used.
  • Cloudinary: The backend sends processed owner/member photos and associated asset identifiers to Cloudinary for image storage and retrieval. These assets are configured as authenticated, rather than intentionally public image uploads. Cloudinary can process the images and service-related technical information.
  • Recovery email delivery: Gmail SMTP through the approved sender pcterajbca7772@gmail.com is prepared locally. Delivery processes the registered email address, one-time recovery-code/message content and provider technical information; password-change notifications contain no password. A controlled test using a disposable local account verified SMTP delivery, and the approved test recipient confirmed receipt of two recovery messages and a password-change notice. This does not verify hosted delivery, provider retention or processing locations. Gmail recovery is not claimed to be available in the hosted service. If you choose to email support or a deletion request, your email provider and the operator's Gmail mailbox handle your sender address and message content. The prepared deletion page opens your email app; it does not itself send email.
  • Device and browser services: The operating system supplies secure storage, app-private storage, camera/photo selection and related system services. Opening a privacy or deletion link launches a browser or offers a copyable link; the visited website and its host can process the request under their own applicable practices.

These providers' actual agreements, subprocessors, processing locations and retention must be checked by the operator. No advertising or third-party analytics SDK is included in the inspected Flutter dependencies. That does not establish what hosting providers, the operator's other systems or a future policy website do. Any additional providers or sharing must be disclosed before publication.

The native mobile sign-in flow uses tokens rather than browser cookies. The backend separately supports browser authentication using a secure, HttpOnly refresh cookie if a web client uses that transport. The public deletion form does not itself create a login session or add an analytics script. Cookies or tracking added by the final website host, content-management system or other operator websites must be confirmed and disclosed as applicable; the app code cannot establish those practices.

6. Security

The release configuration requires the approved HTTPS API. The backend hashes account passwords using Argon2id, stores refresh-token digests rather than plaintext refresh tokens in its authentication records, checks authentication/gym permissions and supports token rotation and revocation. The client uses platform secure storage for its stored refresh credential and restricts authenticated requests to the configured API.

Application diagnostics are designed to avoid logging passwords, bearer/refresh credentials and request bodies. The backend's request logger omits client addresses and raw URLs/bodies; security-event logs can still contain internal identifiers. These application rules do not establish the contents or retention of VPS, reverse-proxy, database, provider or OS logs.

These controls reduce risk; they are not a guarantee of absolute security. The service is not end-to-end encrypted: the backend and authorized service providers process data for the functions described here. This draft does not claim verified encryption at rest for all server databases, backups, logs or local image files.

7. Retention and deletion

For this draft, account and gym data are retained while needed to provide the service and to meet applicable legal obligations. This is the operator's proposed retention principle, not a verified category-by-category retention schedule or a claim that every retained record is legally required. Exact periods, review criteria and implementation still need confirmation.

The implementation keeps gym/business, membership, payment, operational and deletion-request records unless a specific approved process removes them. Financial and recovery safeguards preserve records needed to identify prior transactions and avoid duplicates; marking a membership expired/cancelled or a profile inactive is not erasure of its history.

Authentication/activation records have configured expiry and database cleanup mechanisms. Expiry blocks credential use independently of when background database cleanup removes an expired record. These technical lifetimes are not a general retention period for member, financial or photo data. Local unresolved-operation records and staged photos can persist for recovery. Cloudinary replacement/removal cleanup can be delayed by failures/retries.

Confirmation required — retention: No final retention schedule, backup expiry policy, security-log period, financial-record retention decision or deletion turnaround has been approved for this release. The operator must establish and describe the actual purposes and periods or criteria for each category before publication. This draft makes no fixed-period or legal-compliance claim.

8. Your choices and account-deletion requests

Owners can use implemented profile/record editing and photo controls where available. A member or trainer whose information was entered by a gym should contact that gym about access, correction or removal of their record. For service-level requests, use the operator's confirmed privacy contact below; available rights and any required verification depend on the applicable law and circumstances.

Account holders can open More → Request account deletion in the app. A public request form is also available without installing the app at the existing account-deletion page. The form uses the account email and current password; the in-app flow uses the authenticated account and verifies its current password. For account holders who cannot log in or provide their current password, the prepared local deletion page also offers an email-request option to pcterajbca7772@gmail.com. Email alone does not verify account ownership or complete deletion; identity checks and manual review are still required. This updated page is not yet deployed. Do not send passwords, recovery codes or identity/payment documents in ordinary email or support messages.

A successful in-app or web-form submission records a request and returns a reference. Sending an ordinary email does not automatically create a backend request or return a reference. Request received is not confirmation that deletion has been completed. The current implementation does not automatically delete accounts, gyms, financial records, memberships or audit history. Requests require manual review of identity, the data involved, any justified retention and shared-gym ownership/authorization before removal. Financial records are not automatically erased when a request is received.

9. Children and age-related information

MuscleDesk is intended for adult account holders. Owner signup now requires an adult-account declaration in the app; it is a declaration, not independent age verification. Under-18 member handling remains unavailable until guardian authorization, verification, access controls and documented procedures are implemented. Age checks use submitted or stored dates of birth and are not independent age verification. For profile updates, both the stored date of birth and any submitted replacement are checked; replacing or clearing a stored minor date of birth does not bypass the restriction. The local backend rejects a supplied under-18 date of birth for new member creation and blocks known-minor profile changes, new photo uploads and member-account provisioning/activation, while preserving historical reads, photo removal and financial safeguards. Missing birth dates remain optional and do not establish adulthood; no verified guardian-consent workflow exists. Historical records are not automatically erased.

Confirmation required — age eligibility and minors' records: Verify the interim adult-only workflow in the deployed app/backend, including handling of missing birth dates and existing records. Do not enable under-18 records merely through an age label or checkbox. Guardian authorization/verification, gym responsibilities and the corresponding access and handling controls need implementation and review before that workflow is enabled. No legal-compliance guarantee or independently verified adult identity is claimed.
